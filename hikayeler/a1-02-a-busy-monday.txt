A Busy Monday
A1

Tom wakes up at seven o'clock. He is tired, but he gets up. Today is Monday, and Monday is a busy day.

He goes to the bathroom. He washes his face and brushes his teeth. Then he puts on his school clothes: a white shirt, blue trousers and black shoes.

In the kitchen, he makes his breakfast. He has bread, cheese and a glass of orange juice. He eats very fast.

He takes his school bag and opens the door. It is very quiet outside. There are no cars in the street. There are no children at the bus stop.

"Where is everybody?" Tom thinks.

He waits for the bus. Five minutes. Ten minutes. Twenty minutes. The bus does not come.

His mother opens the window. She is wearing her pyjamas.

"Tom!" she says. "What are you doing?"

"I am waiting for the school bus," he says.

His mother laughs. "Tom, today is Sunday!"

Tom looks at his phone. She is right. It is Sunday.

He goes back into the house, takes off his school clothes and gets into bed again. Sunday is a very good day.
