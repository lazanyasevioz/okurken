A Day at the Zoo
A1

Today, our class goes to the zoo. We go by bus. There are twenty children and two teachers.

The zoo is very big. First, we see the lions. The father lion is sleeping in the sun. He is very lazy. The baby lions are playing with a ball.

Then we see the monkeys. They are funny. One monkey takes a girl's hat and puts it on his head. Everybody laughs, but the girl is not happy. The zookeeper gets the hat back.

Next, we see the elephants. They are very big and grey. One elephant drinks water with its long nose. Then it sprays the water on us! We are all wet.

At twelve o'clock, we eat our lunch near the lake. There are ducks on the lake. We give them some bread.

In the afternoon, we see the giraffes, the zebras and the penguins. The penguins are my favourite animals. They cannot fly, but they swim very fast. They walk like little men in black and white suits.

At three o'clock, we go back to the bus. We are tired and a little wet, but very happy.

On the bus, our teacher asks, "What is your favourite animal?"

My friend Ali says, "The monkey, because he is like me!"
