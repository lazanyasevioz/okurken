The Wrong Train
A2

Kemal was fifteen, and it was his first trip alone. He had to take the train from his town to the city, where his aunt was waiting for him at the station.

The station was very busy. Kemal looked at the big board, found "Platform 4", and got on the train just before the doors closed. He sat by the window, put on his headphones, and listened to music. He was very proud of himself.

After an hour, he looked out of the window. He expected to see tall buildings. Instead, he saw fields, sheep and a lot of water.

"Excuse me," he said to the old man next to him. "When does this train arrive in the city?"

The old man smiled. "This train doesn't go to the city, young man. It goes to the sea."

Kemal's face went white. He took out his phone. The battery was at three percent. He quickly called his aunt.

"Don't worry," she said, and she laughed. "Get off at the last station. I'll come and get you with the car."

The old man's name was Mr Hill. He was a fisherman, and he was going home. For the rest of the trip, he told Kemal stories about storms, big fish and a whale that once followed his boat for a whole day.

At the last station, the air smelled of salt. Kemal's aunt arrived two hours later. She was not angry. She brought sandwiches, and they ate them on the beach while the sun went down.

"So," she said, "how was your first trip alone?"

Kemal thought about it. "It was the wrong train," he said. "But I think it was the right day."

Now, every summer, Kemal takes the same "wrong" train on purpose. Mr Hill always waits for him at the station.
