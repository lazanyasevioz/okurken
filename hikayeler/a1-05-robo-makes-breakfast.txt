Robo Makes Breakfast
A1

The Brown family has a new robot. It is tall and silver, and it has two big blue eyes. Its name is Robo.

"Robo, please make breakfast," says Mrs Brown on Monday morning.

"Yes, Mrs Brown," says Robo.

Robo goes into the kitchen. He opens the fridge. He takes out eggs, milk, bread and butter.

First, he puts the eggs in the toaster. Crack! Then he puts the bread in a cup of hot water. Then he puts butter in the milk.

The family comes into the kitchen. They look at the table.

"Robo," says Mr Brown, "this is not breakfast."

Robo's eyes turn red. "I am sorry," he says. "I do not understand breakfast."

The children laugh. Emma, who is eight, takes Robo's metal hand.

"Come, Robo," she says. "I will teach you."

She shows him: eggs go in a pan, bread goes in the toaster, and milk goes in a glass. Robo watches and learns.

On Tuesday morning, breakfast is perfect: eggs, toast, butter and cold milk.

"Thank you, Robo!" says the family.

Robo's eyes turn green. Then he puts a banana in the toaster.

"Robo!" says everybody.

"Just a joke," says Robo.
