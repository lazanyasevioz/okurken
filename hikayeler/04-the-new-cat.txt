The New Cat
A1

Ali has a new cat. The cat is small and black. It has big green eyes and a white nose.

On the first day, the cat does not eat. It sits under the bed all day. Ali puts some milk next to the bed and waits.

"What is your name?" Ali asks. The cat does not answer. Cats do not talk.

Ali thinks about a name. Maybe "Coal", because it is black? Maybe "Star", because of the white nose? He writes ten names on a paper.

In the evening, the cat comes out. It drinks the milk. Then it walks to the kitchen and jumps on the table. It looks at Ali's dinner: fish and rice.

"No!" says Ali. "That is my fish!"

The cat looks at him. Then it looks at the fish again.

Ali laughs. He gives the cat a small piece of fish. The cat eats it very fast and says, "Meow."

That night, the cat sleeps on Ali's bed, next to his feet. It is warm and soft.

In the morning, Ali takes the paper with the ten names. He does not need it now. He knows the cat's name.

Its name is Fish.
