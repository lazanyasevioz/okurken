The Snowman
A1

In the morning, everything is white. It is snowing!

Ella and her little brother Leo run into the garden. The snow is soft and cold. They make a big ball for the body and a small ball for the head.

They need a face. Ella finds two black stones for the eyes. Leo brings a carrot from the kitchen for the nose. Grandpa gives them his old red hat and his long green scarf.

"He needs a name," says Leo.

"His name is Mr Frost," says Ella.

Every morning, they say "Good morning, Mr Frost!" Every evening, they say "Good night, Mr Frost!" Leo tells him about his day at school.

After a week, the sun comes out. The weather is warm. Mr Frost is smaller every day. On Friday, he is very small. On Saturday, there is only water, two black stones, a carrot, a red hat and a green scarf.

Leo cries. "Where is Mr Frost?"

Grandpa sits next to him. "He is in the ground now," he says. "In spring, he will help the flowers grow. And next winter, he will come back as snow."

Leo puts the red hat in a box under his bed.

"For next winter," he says.
