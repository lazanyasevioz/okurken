The Cooking Video
A2

Ela loved cooking, and she loved watching cooking videos online. One day, she decided to make her own video. She asked her brother Mert to film her with his phone.

"Today, I'm going to show you how to make a perfect chocolate cake," she said to the camera with a big smile.

It did not go well.

First, she opened the bag of flour too quickly, and a white cloud covered the kitchen, the camera and Mert. Then she dropped an egg on the floor and stepped on it. When she tried to use the mixer, she put it on the highest speed, and chocolate flew all over the walls.

Mert was laughing so much that the video was shaking.

The cake went into the oven anyway. Forty minutes later, Ela opened the oven door. The cake was not a cake. It was flat, black on the outside, and wet in the middle.

Ela looked at the camera. She had flour in her hair and chocolate on her nose. She was going to cry, but then she started to laugh instead.

"Well," she said, "today I showed you how NOT to make a chocolate cake. See you next week!"

Mert put the video online that evening. Ela went to bed and forgot about it.

In the morning, her phone was full of messages. The video had fifty thousand views. People loved it. They wrote comments like "This is the most honest cooking video on the internet!" and "Please make another one!"

So she did. Every week, Ela tried a new recipe, and every week, something went wrong. Her videos became very popular. Slowly, her cooking got better too.

After one year, she made the chocolate cake again. This time, it was perfect.

Her followers were very happy for her. But some of them wrote, "We miss the disasters!"
