A Letter in the Wall
A2

The Novak family bought an old house on Elm Street. It needed a lot of work. In the first week, Mr Novak decided to make the kitchen bigger, so he started to break one of the walls.

Behind the wall, there was a small metal box. Inside, there was a letter in a plastic bag. On the envelope, someone had written: "To the person who finds this. From Anna, 10 years old, 1990."

The Novak children, Petra and Jan, read the letter together at the kitchen table.

"Hello! My name is Anna and I live in this house. Today is the 3rd of June, 1990. My favourite food is pizza, and my best friend is my cat, Tiger. I want to be an astronaut when I grow up. What is the world like now? Are there flying cars? Please write back to me if you can!"

At the bottom, there was an address.

"We have to find her," said Petra.

Their mother helped them search on the internet. It took two days, but finally they found a woman called Dr Anna Berg. She lived in another city, and she worked as a scientist for a space agency.

Petra and Jan wrote a letter to her. They told her about the house, the box, and the letter. They also answered her questions: "There are no flying cars yet. But there are phones that are also cameras, and you can talk to people in other countries with video. Pizza is still everybody's favourite."

Two weeks later, a big package arrived. Inside, there was a letter from Anna, a photo of her as a little girl with a fat orange cat, and a small model of a rocket.

"Thank you for finding my letter," she wrote. "I didn't become an astronaut, but I help to send astronauts to space. Now, please put your own letter in the wall, for the next children."

And they did.
