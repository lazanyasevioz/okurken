The Broken Window
A2

It was the last day of the summer holidays. Burak and his friends were playing football in the street. Burak took a shot, but the ball went too high. It flew over the wall and crashed through the window of Mr Aydın's house.

For a second, nobody moved. Then all the boys ran away, and Burak ran with them.

Mr Aydın was the oldest man in the street. He lived alone with a big grey dog and he never smiled. All the children were a little afraid of him.

That night, Burak couldn't sleep. He kept thinking about the broken window. It was cold at night, and Mr Aydın was old. And it was Burak's ball. Everybody knew it.

In the morning, Burak took his money box. He had saved forty pounds for a new video game. He put the money in his pocket and walked to Mr Aydın's house. His legs were shaking when he knocked on the door.

The big grey dog barked. Then the door opened. Mr Aydın looked down at him.

"I broke your window," said Burak quickly. "I'm sorry. I ran away, and that was wrong. Here is forty pounds. It's all my money."

Mr Aydın looked at the money for a long time. Then he did something Burak had never seen. He smiled.

"Keep your money," he said. "The window costs more than forty pounds. But you can help me. I need someone to walk my dog. My legs are not very good now."

So Burak walked the dog, whose name was Bulut, every afternoon for a month. Sometimes Mr Aydın came too, and he told Burak about his life. He was a famous footballer when he was young, and he still had his old photos and medals.

At the end of the month, Mr Aydın gave Burak a present. It was an old leather football, signed by his whole team from 1975.

"For someone who knows how to say sorry," he said.
