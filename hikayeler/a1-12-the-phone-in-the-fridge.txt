The Phone in the Fridge
A1

Mr Jones is a good man, but he forgets things. He forgets his keys. He forgets his glasses. He forgets birthdays.

This morning, he cannot find his phone.

"Where is my phone?" he says. He looks on the table. He looks in his coat. He looks in the car. He looks in the bathroom. No phone.

He takes his wife's phone and calls his number. He listens. He can hear his phone. It is ringing, but the sound is very quiet and very cold.

He follows the sound. It comes from the kitchen. He opens the cupboard. No. He opens the oven. No. He opens the fridge.

His phone is in the fridge, next to the milk and the cheese!

"How is my phone in the fridge?" he asks.

His wife thinks for a moment. "Last night, you had a glass of milk and you talked on the phone," she says. "Then you put the milk on the table and the phone in the fridge."

Mr Jones laughs. He takes the phone. It is very cold.

"Now, where is the milk?" asks his wife.

They look on the table. It is not there. They look everywhere.

The milk is in the bathroom, next to the toothbrush.
