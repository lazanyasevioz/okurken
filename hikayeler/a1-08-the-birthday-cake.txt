The Birthday Cake
A1

Today is Mum's birthday. She is forty. Dad and the children want to make a big cake for her.

Mum is at work. They have four hours.

Dad reads the recipe. "We need flour, sugar, eggs, butter and chocolate," he says.

Anna is ten. She puts the flour and sugar in a big bowl. Ben is six. He breaks the eggs. One egg goes in the bowl, and one egg goes on the floor.

"Oops," says Ben.

They mix everything and put the cake in the oven. The kitchen smells very good.

After forty minutes, the cake is ready. It is big and brown and beautiful. Anna puts chocolate on top. Ben writes "MUM" with small sweets.

At six o'clock, they hear the car. Mum is home!

Dad takes the cake to the door. But Max, the cat, runs between his feet. Dad falls, and the cake falls too. Now the cake is on the floor, in a hundred pieces.

Everybody is quiet. Then the door opens.

Mum looks at the floor. She looks at Dad. She looks at the children with chocolate on their faces.

Then she starts to laugh. She sits on the floor, takes a spoon and eats a piece of cake.

"This is the best birthday cake in the world," she says.
