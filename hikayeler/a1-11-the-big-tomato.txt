The Big Tomato
A1

My grandpa has a small garden behind his house. He grows potatoes, carrots, beans and tomatoes. Every summer, I stay with him for two weeks.

This year, there is a competition in the village: "The Biggest Tomato". The prize is a gold cup.

"We can win," says Grandpa.

Every morning, we go to the garden early. We give water to the tomatoes. We talk to them. Grandpa says that tomatoes like music, so I sing to them. My singing is not very good, but the tomatoes don't complain.

One tomato is bigger than the others. It is very big and very red. We call it Bob.

On the day of the competition, we carry Bob to the village in a box. There are a lot of tomatoes on the table. Mr White's tomato is big. Mrs Green's tomato is very big. But Bob is the biggest.

A man puts Bob on the scale. "One kilo and two hundred grams!" he says. "The winner is Bob!"

Everybody claps. Grandpa gets the gold cup, and I get a photo in the village newspaper.

That evening, we make a big salad with Bob.

"Was Bob a good tomato?" asks Grandpa.

"Bob was a delicious tomato," I say.
