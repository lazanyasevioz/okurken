The New Girl
A1

There is a new girl in my class. Her name is Lucia. She is from Spain. She has long black hair and a yellow bag.

On her first day, she sits alone. She does not speak a lot of English. At lunch, she eats her sandwich alone under a tree.

I want to talk to her, but I don't speak Spanish. I only know one word: "hola". It means "hello".

The next day, I sit next to her at lunch.

"Hola," I say.

She looks at me, and she smiles. "Hello," she says in English.

I show her my lunch: an apple and a cheese sandwich. "Apple," I say.

"Manzana," she says.

"Sandwich," I say.

"Sándwich," she says, and we laugh. It is almost the same word.

Every day, we sit together under the tree. I teach her English words, and she teaches me Spanish words. Water is "agua". Friend is "amigo" or "amiga". Book is "libro".

After two months, her English is very good. My Spanish is not very good, but it is not bad.

Today, our teacher asks, "Who is your best friend?"

Lucia and I say the same thing at the same time: "Mi amiga!"
