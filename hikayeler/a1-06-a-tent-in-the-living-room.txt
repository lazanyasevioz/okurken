A Tent in the Living Room
A1

It is Saturday, and it is raining. It is raining a lot. Sam and his sister Mia are at home. They are bored.

"We can't go to the park," says Mia.

"We can't play football," says Sam.

They watch the rain. They read a book. They play a game. It is only ten o'clock.

Then Sam has an idea. "Let's go camping!" he says.

"In the rain?" asks Mia.

"No," says Sam. "In the living room!"

They take four chairs and two big blankets. They put the blankets over the chairs. Now they have a tent! They put pillows and a lamp inside it.

Their dad comes in. "What is this?" he asks.

"It's our camp," says Mia. "Do you want to come in?"

Dad smiles. He goes to the kitchen and comes back with hot chocolate and biscuits. He sits in the tent with them. He is very big, and the tent is very small. One chair falls down, and everybody laughs.

In the afternoon, Dad tells them a story with the lamp under his face. It is a little scary.

In the evening, the rain stops. The sun comes out.

"Do you want to go to the park now?" asks Dad.

"No," say Sam and Mia. "We are camping."
