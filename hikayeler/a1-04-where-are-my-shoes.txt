Where Are My Shoes?
A1

Lily has a new dog. His name is Max. He is brown and white, and he is very happy all the time.

One morning, Lily cannot find her left shoe. She looks under the bed. She looks in the cupboard. She looks behind the door. It is not there.

"Mum, where is my shoe?" she asks.

"I don't know," says her mum. "Look again."

The next day, her dad cannot find his sock. Then her brother cannot find his football boot. Then her mum cannot find her slipper.

"This is very strange," says Dad.

On Saturday, Lily plays in the garden. Max runs to the old tree at the end of the garden. He starts to dig.

Lily follows him. Under the tree, there is a big hole. In the hole, there are a lot of things: her left shoe, Dad's sock, her brother's football boot, Mum's slipper, two tennis balls and an old toy car.

"Max!" says Lily. "You are a thief!"

Max looks at her. He moves his tail and gives her the shoe. He is very proud.

Now the family has a new rule: all shoes go in a box with a lid. Max is not happy about the new rule.
