First Day at the Café
A2

Jake was very nervous on his first day at the Blue Moon Café. He was eighteen, and it was his first real job.

The manager, Mrs Lee, gave him a black apron and a notebook. "Write down every order," she said. "Smile, be polite, and don't run."

At nine o'clock, the café was quiet. Jake made two cups of tea and one hot chocolate, and nothing went wrong. He started to feel better.

At twelve o'clock, everything changed. Suddenly, every table was full. People wanted coffee, sandwiches, soup and cake, and they all wanted them now.

Jake wrote very fast, but his notebook became a mess. He gave a cheese sandwich to a man who wanted chicken. He gave a cappuccino to a woman who wanted green tea. He forgot table seven completely.

Then he saw an important-looking man in a grey suit. Jake walked quickly to his table with a large coffee. He did not run, but he walked very, very fast. His foot hit a chair, and the coffee flew through the air and landed on the man's suit.

The café went silent.

Jake wanted to disappear. "I'm so sorry," he said. "I'm really, really sorry."

The man looked at his suit. Then, to Jake's surprise, he laughed.

"On my first day at work," the man said, "I dropped a whole cake on my boss. Don't worry about it."

Later, Jake found out who the man was. He was Mr Lee, the owner of the café, and Mrs Lee's husband.

At the end of the day, Mrs Lee gave Jake his first pay. "You made a lot of mistakes," she said. "But you said sorry, you didn't stop, and you smiled all day. See you tomorrow."
