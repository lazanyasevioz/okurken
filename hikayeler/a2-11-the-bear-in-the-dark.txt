The Bear in the Dark
A2

Last summer, my friend Arda and I went camping in the mountains for the first time. We were both sixteen. We had a small tent, two sleeping bags, a lot of chocolate, and no experience at all.

The first problem was the tent. The instructions were in German, and we didn't speak German. It took us two hours to put it up. When we finished, it looked more like a sad sandwich than a tent, but we were proud of it.

The second problem was dinner. We tried to make a fire, but the wood was wet. In the end, we ate cold beans from the can and a lot of chocolate.

The third problem came at midnight.

I woke up because I heard something outside. It was big and heavy. I could hear it walking slowly around the tent. Then it started breathing very loudly, right next to my head.

"Arda," I whispered. "Wake up. There's a bear."

Arda opened his eyes. We both lay very still. We were too afraid to move. The animal pushed the side of the tent with its head. Then it made a long, deep noise.

"Mooooo."

Arda and I looked at each other. Very slowly, I opened the door of the tent and looked out with my torch.

A big black and white cow was standing there. She was eating the grass next to our tent and looking at us with great interest. Behind her, there were twenty more cows. We were in the middle of a farmer's field.

We laughed so much that we couldn't sleep again. In the morning, the farmer came on his tractor. He was not angry. He gave us fresh milk and showed us the real campsite. It was only five hundred metres away, with toilets, showers and a small shop that sold hot food.

We still go camping every summer. And every time we hear a noise in the night, one of us says quietly, "Is it a bear?" and the other one answers, "Mooooo."
