The Little Fish
A1

Nora has a little orange fish. His name is Goldie. He lives in a glass bowl on her desk.

Every day, Nora gives him food. Every day, Goldie swims in a circle. Round and round and round.

One day, Nora watches him for a long time. "Are you happy, Goldie?" she asks. "Your house is very small."

Goldie does not answer. He swims round and round.

Nora goes to her mother. "Goldie's bowl is too small," she says. "He needs a bigger home."

Her mother thinks. "There is a pond in Grandma's garden," she says. "It is big, and there are other fish in it."

On Sunday, they take Goldie to Grandma's house. They carry him in a bag of water. Nora holds the bag very carefully in the car.

Grandma's pond is big and green. There are plants and stones, and there are five other orange fish.

Nora opens the bag. Goldie looks at the pond. Then he swims out. He does not swim in a circle. He swims straight to the other fish, under a big green leaf.

Nora is a little sad, but she is happy too.

"You can visit him every Sunday," says Grandma.

And she does. Every Sunday, Goldie comes to the top of the water to say hello.
